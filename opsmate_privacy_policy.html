IIntroduction

OpsMate (hereinafter referred to as "the App", developed and maintained by the OpsMate Team) is a standalone, client-side MySQL database management tool. Our core engineering principle is zero data collection: we do not collect, store, track, or transmit any of your personal data or operational records to any of our servers or unconfigured third-party platforms. This Privacy Policy outlines the data handling rules, security boundaries, and user rights of the App. Please read this document thoroughly. By downloading, accessing, or using the App, you acknowledge and agree to the terms described herein.

引引言

OpsMate（以下简称“本应用”，由 OpsMate 团队开发与维护）是一款纯本地运行的 MySQL 数据库移动端管理工具。我们的核心产品设计原则为零数据收集：绝不收集、不追踪、不存储、不上传任何用户的个人数据或操作记录至我们自建的服务器或未经授权的第三方平台。本隐私协议旨在向您明确本应用的数据处理边界、安全机制及用户权利。在下载、访问或使用本应用前，请您仔细阅读本协议全部内容；您的使用行为即视为完全知悉并同意本协议各条款。

1Zero Personal Information Collection

The App does not collect, track, or harvest any personal information, specifically including:

  • No Identity Information: We do not collect names, phone numbers, email addresses, national IDs, or social accounts;
  • No Hardware Identifiers: We do not collect device serial numbers, IMEI, MAC addresses, IDFA/GAID, or operating system fingerprinting data;
  • No Behavioral Analytics: We do not track navigation paths, screen interaction frequencies, SQL query histories, or geo-location;
  • No Tracking SDKs: We do not integrate third-party advertising, commercial tracking, behavioral analytics, or user profiling SDKs.

一零个人信息收集承诺

本应用不收集、不追踪、不索取任何形式的个人隐私信息，具体包括：

  • 无身份凭证收集：不收集姓名、手机号码、电子邮箱、身份证号或任何第三方社交账号信息；
  • 无设备指纹采集：不采集设备序列号、IMEI、MAC 地址、广告标识符（IDFA/GAID）或系统硬件指纹；
  • 无行为日志埋点：不监控界面操作轨迹、查询频率、SQL 执行偏好或地理位置；
  • 无第三方追踪 SDK：不嵌入任何第三方商业广告 SDK、行为统计分析或用户画像构建工具。

2Local Data Storage & Cryptographic Protection

All operational data created or configured within the App resides exclusively on your local device. We have neither the technical capability nor the legal right to access, inspect, modify, or exfiltrate your local data:

  • Database Connection Profiles: MySQL host IP/domain, port, username, connection labels, and connection passwords entered by you;
  • Query & Scratch Records: SQL execution statements, saved code snippets, query results, and local table configurations;
  • AI Configuration Keys: AI endpoints, custom models, and API keys that you manually provide;
  • Custom Preferences: Theme choices, shortcut layouts, and local UI preferences.

Hardware-Backed Local Encryption: Sensitive credentials (including database passwords and private AI API Keys) are encrypted inside the application's local sandbox using system-level secure storage primitives (such as Apple Keychain on iOS and KeyStore/EncryptedSharedPreferences on Android) coupled with AES-256 standard encryption.

Data Erasure: You retain full sovereignty over your data. You may irrevocably erase all locally stored data at any time either via the in-app "Reset / Clear All Data" settings or by directly uninstalling the App from your device.

二本地数据存储与高强度加密保护

您在使用本应用过程中产生或配置的所有数据均仅留存在您的本地设备中。我们既无技术途径、亦无法律权限读取、审查、修改或转储您的任何数据：

  • 数据库连接配置：您手动填写的 MySQL 服务器地址/域名、端口、用户名、连接别名以及连接密码；
  • 操作与收藏记录：您编写并执行的 SQL 语句、SQL 收藏片段（Snippets）、查询结果缓存及表格展示偏好；
  • AI 服务配置：您自行录入的 AI 服务商地址、自定义模型名称及私有 API Key；
  • 应用环境设置：主题偏好、快捷操作栏设置等个性化本地选项。

系统级安全加密保障：对于数据库连接密码、私有 API Key 等敏感凭据，本应用采用系统硬件级加密设施（如 iOS Keychain、Android KeyStore）并结合 AES-256 算法在本地应用沙箱内加密持久化，杜绝明文泄露风险。

数据自主销毁：您对自身数据享有完全的自主权。您可随时在应用内通过「设置 - 清空/重置所有数据」功能彻底销毁本地存储，亦可通过卸载本应用的方式永久抹除全部本地数据。

3Special Notice on Third-Party AI Features & Data Sharing

  • Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) Architecture: The AI features provided in the App function strictly as a client-side interface. You independently configure your own AI service credentials (such as an API Key from OpenAI, DeepSeek, Anthropic, or compatible custom endpoints). We neither provide nor sell AI subscriptions, nor do we levy fees on AI interactions;
  • Disclosed Data Sent & Explicit Purposes: When you actively use the AI features, only the minimum data necessary to process your request is sent:
    • User Prompts: Natural language questions or instructions you input;
    • Table Schema Metadata: Table and column structure definitions (DDL) from your selected database, used solely to assist the AI model in generating syntactically valid and context-accurate SQL;
    • Error Diagnostics: SQL execution error messages when troubleshooting failed queries.
    All data sent is strictly used for the immediate purpose of generating SQL, explaining queries, or analyzing diagnostic errors within your active session.
  • Direct End-to-End Transmission: All user prompts and schema context are dispatched directly from your mobile device to your designated third-party AI service provider over an encrypted HTTPS connection. No traffic is routed through, collected, inspected, or retained on any of our servers;
  • In-App Prior Consent Requirement: Before any data is transmitted to a third-party AI service for the first time, the App presents an in-app notice disclosing the exact data being sent and the recipient, and explicitly obtains your permission. If you decline, no data will be transmitted;
  • Confirmation of Equivalent Protection by Third Parties: The App confirms and requires that any third-party AI service provider (including OpenAI, DeepSeek, Anthropic, and other supported providers) with whom data is shared provides the same or equal protection of user data as set forth in this Privacy Policy under their respective enterprise and API data privacy terms. We never authorize third-party AI providers to use your confidential database queries or schemas for public model training.

三第三方 AI 辅助功能与数据共享特别说明

  • 用户自主配置（BYOK）架构：本应用内置的 AI 功能仅作为纯客户端调用入口。您需要自行取得并配置相应 AI 服务商的 API 访问凭据（如 OpenAI、DeepSeek、Anthropic 或兼容协议的大模型）。我们不销售任何 AI 算力服务，亦不就 AI 功能收取中转费用；
  • 明示传输的数据内容与具体用途：当您主动使用 AI 辅助功能时，仅向第三方模型发送实现该功能所必需的最小数据项：
    • 用户提示词（Prompts）：您在输入框中主动填写的自然语言提问或指令；
    • 表结构元数据（Schema）：您当前所关联数据库的表名、列名与 DDL 定义，仅用于辅助大模型理解业务上下文并生成语法正确的准确 SQL；
    • 报错与执行诊断信息：SQL 执行失败时的错误提示，用于请求 AI 进行诊断与调优建议。
    上述数据仅在当前会话中用于即时生成 SQL、解释查询与定位错误，绝不作任何其他用途。
  • 端到端直接加密通信：当发起 AI 交互时，您的输入与表结构上下文均由您的本地设备通过加密通道（HTTPS）直接发往您所指定的第三方 AI 服务商，绝不经过我们的任何中继服务器，我们亦无技术途径读取、留存或转储该数据；
  • 应用内前置知情与授权同意：在向第三方 AI 服务商发起首次数据传输前，本应用会在应用内弹出专门的知情通知，明示所传输的数据内容及接收方，并在取得您的明确授权同意后方才执行发送。若您拒绝授权，本应用绝不会向第三方发起任何数据通信；
  • 第三方同等安全保护确认：本应用确认并要求与本应用发生数据交互的任何第三方 AI 服务商（包括但不限于 OpenAI、DeepSeek、Anthropic 等）均在其商业及 API 隐私条款中提供与本隐私协议同等或等效的数据安全保护水平。本应用绝不授权任何第三方服务商将您的核心业务数据、提示词或库表结构用于模型公开训练。

4System Permissions

The App requests only the strictly minimal operating system permissions necessary to deliver core client functionalities:

  • Network Access Permission: Utilized solely to establish direct TCP/TLS connections to your designated MySQL database instances and to perform outbound HTTPS requests to your configured AI endpoints. It is never used for background silent tracking or analytical telemetry.
  • Biometric Authentication Permission (Face ID / Touch ID / Fingerprint): Utilized exclusively for verifying your identity when you opt to enable the "App Lock" protection feature. This authentication is executed natively by your device operating system (e.g., iOS LocalAuthentication, Android BiometricPrompt). The App receives solely a boolean verification result ("Success" or "Failed") and never reads, processes, stores, or transmits your actual facial contours, fingerprint templates, or biometric credentials.

You may grant or revoke these permissions at any time via your device operating system settings. Revoking network permissions will prevent remote database connectivity, but your locally stored configurations will remain accessible.

四系统权限调用说明

本应用遵循最小必要原则，仅申请实现核心功能所必需的系统权限：

  • 网络访问权限（Network Permission）：仅用于直接建立通往您指定的 MySQL 数据库服务器的 TCP/TLS 直连，以及向您自主配置的第三方 AI 接口发送 HTTPS 请求。绝不用于任何静默上传或后台数据统计。
  • 生物识别认证权限（Face ID / Touch ID / 指纹识别）：仅用于您主动开启「应用安全锁」时进行本地身份鉴权。该验证完全由操作系统底层安全框架接管（如 iOS LocalAuthentication、Android BiometricPrompt），本应用仅接收系统返回的“验证通过/失败”布尔结果，绝不会读取、收集、留存或上传您的面容图像、指纹原始特征等任何生物识别数据。

您可随时在操作系统「设置」中查看并自主调整以上权限。关闭网络权限将导致无法建立数据库直连和 AI 交互，但不影响您查看与管理本地已存记录。
